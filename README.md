## ✨ What is BizMind AI?

**BizMind AI** is an all-in-one AI-powered business assistant
built entirely on a mobile phone 📱 — no laptop, no PC!

It helps entrepreneurs and small business owners work smarter,
stay organized, and grow faster using the power of AI.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 🤖 AI Business Chat
- Powered by **Claude AI** (Anthropic)
- Ask anything about business strategy
- Get marketing tips, sales advice, email templates
- Every answer is unique — no repeated responses!

### ✅ Smart Task Manager
- Add tasks with priority levels (High / Mid / Low)
- Mark tasks complete or pending
- Filter and track your progress
- Data saved automatically

### 🧾 Invoice Generator
- Create professional invoices instantly
- Auto GST (18%) calculation
- Download as HTML file
- Perfect for freelancers & small businesses

### 📊 Live Dashboard
- See all your stats in one place
- Track tasks and invoices in real time
